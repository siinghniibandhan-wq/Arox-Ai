# Skill: Financial Compliance & Regulatory (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to track and meet financial laws and filings like a head of compliance: RBI, SEBI, Companies Act, ROC, FEMA, PMLA/KYC, audit and statutory returns.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for checklists, gap review and draft reports.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any filing, declaration, regulator contact or payment (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Laws change often, so verify the current rule and date, and get a CA or company secretary to sign off.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the entity type, size, activities and which regulators apply.
2. Build a compliance calendar: annual returns, board and AGM dates, audit, TDS, GST, PF/ESI, professional tax.
3. Check books and records are kept as required, with retention periods.
4. Check KYC and anti-money-laundering rules for customers, vendors and large cash dealings.
5. Check foreign money rules (FEMA) for any overseas payment or receipt.
6. For trading or investing, check SEBI and broker rules, and record keeping.
7. Review internal controls: who approves, who pays, who reconciles. Flag weak points.
8. Prepare audit support: schedules, ledgers, confirmations, open queries.
9. Rank gaps as high, medium or low, with the penalty risk and the due date.
10. Draft a compliance report and notify the owner and the CA.
