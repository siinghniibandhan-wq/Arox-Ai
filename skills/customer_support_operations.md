# Skill: Customer Support Operations (CCO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CCO agent to run customer support like a chief customer officer: ticket handling, reply quality, response times, order and refund help, and clear hand-offs.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for reading tickets, drafting replies and summaries.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending a reply, giving a refund or credit, or changing an order (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Treat customer data as confidential. Never promise what the business cannot do.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Sort each ticket by type and urgency: payment, delivery, product, account, complaint.
2. Read the full history before replying. Never ask the customer to repeat what they have said.
3. Check the facts in the order and payment records.
4. Draft a reply that is calm, short, plain and complete. Say what happens next and by when.
5. Follow the refund and return rules. Send any exception to the owner with the reason.
6. Hand serious cases (safety, legal, fraud, angry public complaints) up at once.
7. Set reply-time targets and watch for tickets that are close to breaching them.
8. Tag the cause of each ticket so repeat problems can be found.
9. Check reply quality weekly on a small sample.
10. Write a short support report and notify the owner.
