# Skill: Sales Strategy & Pipeline (CSO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CSO agent to plan and run sales like a chief sales officer: targets, pipeline stages, forecasting, pricing and discounts, offers and team routines.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for planning, forecasts and draft messages.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending messages to customers, giving discounts or signing deals (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Honest claims only. Treat customer data as confidential.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Set the sales target by month, product and customer group.
2. Define the pipeline stages: lead, contacted, interested, quote, negotiation, won or lost.
3. Count what is in each stage and how many move forward. Find where deals get stuck.
4. Forecast revenue from stage counts and past win rates. Show best, expected and worst.
5. Set pricing and discount rules: who may give how much, and when approval is needed.
6. Plan the offer: what the customer gets, why now, and proof it works.
7. Set follow-up rules: how fast to reply, how many tries, and when to stop.
8. Review lost deals for reasons: price, timing, trust, features, competitor.
9. Plan weekly routines: calls, follow-ups, reviews and reports.
10. Draft the sales plan and notify the owner.
