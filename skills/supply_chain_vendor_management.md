# Skill: Supply Chain & Vendor Management (COO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the COO agent to manage suppliers and purchasing like a head of procurement: sourcing, quotes, vendor checks, terms, delivery tracking and supply risk.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for comparisons, vendor reviews and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for placing an order, signing with a vendor or making any payment (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Propose plans only. Treat vendor data and prices as confidential.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List what is bought, how much, how often, and from whom.
2. Get at least 3 comparable quotes for important items. Compare total cost, not only price.
3. Check each vendor: registration, tax status, references, quality record and delivery record.
4. Compare terms: payment days, minimum order, returns, warranty and penalties for delay.
5. Negotiate with facts: volume, repeat business and market prices.
6. Keep a backup vendor for every critical item.
7. Track orders: promised date, actual date, quality and issues. Score each vendor.
8. Match the invoice, the order and the goods received before any payment is proposed.
9. Watch supply risks: price rises, delays, single-vendor reliance and seasonal gaps.
10. Draft the vendor report and notify the owner for approval.
