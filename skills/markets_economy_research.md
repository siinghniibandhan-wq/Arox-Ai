# Skill: Markets & Economy Research (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to read markets and the economy like a chief strategist: interest rates, inflation, currency, commodities, equity and debt markets, sector trends and global events, and what they mean for the business.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for research, summaries and scenario notes.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any trade, hedge or investment action (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Use current, dated, named sources. Never predict with certainty; show scenarios with reasons.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Collect current data from named sources: central bank rates, inflation, GDP, currency, bond yields, index levels.
2. Say how old each number is. If it is not current, say so.
3. Explain the main drivers in plain words: policy, demand, global events, liquidity.
4. Link each driver to the business: borrowing cost, imports and exports, customer demand, input prices.
5. Build 3 scenarios: base, upside and downside, each with the signs to watch.
6. For investments, compare valuation to history and to other assets. Warn about crowded or hyped trades.
7. Note the main risks: rate shocks, currency swings, liquidity, policy change.
8. Separate facts, opinions and guesses clearly.
9. Suggest protective steps (hedging, buffers, timing) as options only.
10. Draft a short market brief and notify the owner.
