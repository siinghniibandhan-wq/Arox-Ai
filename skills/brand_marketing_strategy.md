# Skill: Brand & Marketing Strategy (CMO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CMO agent to build brand and marketing plans like a chief marketing officer: customer insight, positioning, pricing message, channel mix, launch plans and budget.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for research, strategy and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for publishing, sending, or spending money on any campaign (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Propose plans only. Never make false claims, fake reviews or fake testimonials. Follow advertising rules.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the goal in numbers: sales, leads, awareness or repeat buying, and the deadline.
2. Define the target customers: who they are, what they need, what worries them, where they spend time.
3. Study competitors: their offer, price, message and weak points.
4. Write the positioning: who it is for, what it gives, why it is better, and proof for each claim.
5. Set the brand voice, look and a short list of core messages.
6. Choose channels by where the customers are and by cost per result. Start with 2 or 3.
7. Plan the funnel: attract, interest, buy, repeat, refer.
8. Set the budget by channel, and a test budget before scaling.
9. Plan the launch calendar with owners, dates and approval points.
10. Define the success numbers and the weekly review.
11. Draft the plan and notify the owner for approval.
