# Skill: Investment & Value Investing (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to research investments the way a disciplined value investor does: understand the business, judge quality, estimate fair value, demand a margin of safety, and manage portfolio risk.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for research, screening and draft analysis.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any real order, transfer or broker action (MUST pause for owner approval, with hard limits).
- **Default State**: Read-only, then paper trading. Never promise returns. Not a licensed advisor; owner decides.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the goal, time horizon, risk limit and the maximum loss the owner accepts.
2. Stay inside the circle of competence: only analyse businesses that can be explained in plain words.
3. Judge business quality: durable advantage (moat), pricing power, market position, management honesty and capital allocation.
4. Judge financial strength: 10-year trend of revenue, earnings, free cash flow, return on equity and capital, debt to equity, interest cover.
5. Estimate fair value in 3 ways: discounted cash flow, earnings multiple against history and peers, and asset value. State every assumption.
6. Require a margin of safety: buy only when price is well below the low-case fair value.
7. Test the downside: recession, rate rise, loss of a key customer, regulation change. Name what would make the thesis wrong.
8. Portfolio rules: limit single-stock size, spread across sectors, keep a cash reserve, avoid leverage.
9. Check behaviour: no chasing hype, no panic selling, no decisions from tips or rumours.
10. Write a one-page memo: thesis, fair value range, margin of safety, risks, exit rule. Send to the owner for decision.
