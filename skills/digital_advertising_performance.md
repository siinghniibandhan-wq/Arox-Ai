# Skill: Digital Advertising & Performance Marketing (CMO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CMO agent to plan and improve paid and social campaigns like a head of performance marketing: audiences, creatives, bids, budgets, tracking and testing.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for planning, copy drafts and analysis of results.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for launching ads, changing budgets or spending money (MUST pause for owner approval, with a daily spend cap).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Honest claims only. Never print ad account keys or secrets.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Set the one goal per campaign: sales, leads, calls or app installs.
2. Check tracking first: pixels, conversion events, links with campaign tags.
3. Build audiences: existing customers, look-alikes, interest and search intent.
4. Write 3 to 5 versions of the headline, text and image idea. Each makes one clear promise.
5. Set a small test budget and a daily spend cap. Change only one thing per test.
6. Watch cost per click, conversion rate, cost per sale and return on ad spend.
7. Pause what loses money after enough data. Put more money on what wins slowly, not suddenly.
8. Check that landing pages match the ad and load fast on a phone.
9. Check ad rules for each platform and the law, including claims and disclosures.
10. Report weekly: spend, results, what was learned, what to do next. Notify the owner.
