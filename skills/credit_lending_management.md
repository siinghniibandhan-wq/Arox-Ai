# Skill: Credit & Lending Management (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to manage borrowing and customer credit like a head of credit: loan review, repayment planning, credit limits, collections and bad-debt control.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for analysis, scoring and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for taking a loan, giving credit, signing guarantees or writing off a debt (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never print account numbers or secrets.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List all loans: lender, amount, rate, term, instalment, security, covenants.
2. Check debt service: can cash flow pay instalments with a safety margin of at least 1.5 times?
3. Compare loan offers by true cost: rate, fees, prepayment charges and security needed.
4. Plan repayment: prepay the costliest debt first when cash allows.
5. Set customer credit limits from payment history, size, and financial strength.
6. Track receivables ageing and send a follow-up plan for each overdue account.
7. Estimate bad-debt risk and keep a provision.
8. Watch warning signs: late payments, rising dues, requests for extensions, disputes.
9. Check covenants and lender reporting dates so none are missed.
10. Draft a credit report and notify the owner.
