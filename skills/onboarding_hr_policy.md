# Skill: Onboarding & HR Policy (CHRO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CHRO agent to set up people processes like a head of HR operations: onboarding, employee records, leave, attendance, handbook and policies.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for checklists, policy drafts and record checks.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for changing employee records, pay, contracts or any policy that applies to staff (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Employee records are confidential; show only what a person needs to see.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Prepare the onboarding checklist: documents, bank details, tools, access, introductions.
2. Create the first-week plan with a named buddy and clear first goals.
3. Keep employee records complete and correct, and store them safely.
4. Set leave, attendance, holiday and work-hours rules in simple words.
5. Draft or update the handbook: conduct, safety, data use, expenses, grievance steps.
6. Check each policy against current labour laws, and mark what needs legal review.
7. Plan the probation review with clear targets and dates.
8. Plan the exit process: notice, handover, access removal, dues and documents.
9. Keep a calendar of renewals, reviews and policy dates.
10. Draft the pack and notify the owner for approval.
