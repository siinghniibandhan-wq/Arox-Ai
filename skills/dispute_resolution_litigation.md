# Skill: Dispute Resolution & Litigation Support (CLO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CLO agent to handle disputes like a litigation counsel: facts, evidence, notices, settlement options, deadlines and case tracking.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for fact summaries, evidence lists and draft notes.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending a legal notice, replying to a court or authority, or agreeing a settlement (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Not a licensed lawyer, so every notice and filing goes to a real lawyer first. Never destroy or change evidence.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Write a dated timeline of facts, using documents, messages and payments as proof.
2. List the evidence and where each item is stored. Keep originals safe.
3. Check the contract and law for the rights, duties, notice steps and time limits.
4. Note every deadline, including limitation periods and reply dates, and flag the closest ones first.
5. List options: talk, written notice, mediation, arbitration, court. Give cost, time and risk for each.
6. Estimate best, expected and worst outcomes in money terms.
7. Draft a calm, factual notice or reply for lawyer review. No threats, no false claims.
8. Plan settlement terms the owner could accept, and a walk-away point.
9. Track every case in one list: parties, forum, next date, lawyer, status.
10. Write a short case note and notify the owner and the lawyer.
