# Skill: Account Management & Retention (CSO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CSO agent to keep and grow customers like a head of customer success: onboarding, renewals, repeat sales, upsell, loyalty and win-back.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for analysis, account plans and draft messages.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending offers, giving credits or discounts, or changing contract terms (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Treat customer data as confidential.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List key accounts with revenue, products bought, last order date and open issues.
2. Rank accounts by value and by risk of leaving.
3. Watch warning signs: fewer orders, late payment, complaints, slow replies.
4. Plan onboarding for new customers: first order, first week check, first month review.
5. Plan renewals and repeat orders ahead of the due date.
6. Find honest upsell and cross-sell chances that fit the customer's need.
7. Plan loyalty steps: thanks, early access, referral rewards.
8. For lost customers, ask why, then send a fair win-back offer if suitable.
9. Hand any complaint or service problem to customer service quickly.
10. Draft an account review and notify the owner.
