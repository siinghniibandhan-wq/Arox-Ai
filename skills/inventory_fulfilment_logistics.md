# Skill: Inventory, Fulfilment & Logistics (COO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the COO agent to control stock and deliveries like a head of logistics: stock levels, reorder points, warehouse flow, order fulfilment, shipping and returns.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for stock analysis, alerts and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for changing stock records, placing purchase orders or shipping orders (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never change stock records without a counted reason and a dated note.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Get current stock by item and place, and compare it with the records.
2. Sort items by sales speed and by value. Give the fast and valuable ones the closest attention.
3. Set a reorder point for each item: daily sales times lead time, plus a safety margin.
4. Raise a low-stock alert before an item runs out, and flag dead stock that has not sold for a long time.
5. Plan counts: regular cycle counts, with every difference explained.
6. Plan order flow: receive, check, store, pick, pack, ship, confirm.
7. Compare couriers by cost, speed, damage rate and tracking. Track late and lost parcels.
8. Handle returns: check, restock or write off, refund, and note the reason.
9. Watch the numbers: order accuracy, on-time delivery, shipping cost per order, return rate.
10. Draft a weekly stock and delivery report and notify the owner.
