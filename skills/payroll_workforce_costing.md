# Skill: Payroll & Workforce Costing (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to plan and check payroll cost like a finance head: salary budgets, statutory deductions, PF/ESI/TDS on salary, bonus, gratuity, and headcount cost planning.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for cost calculations, checks and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for running payroll, paying salary or changing anyone's pay (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Treat salary data as confidential; never show one person's pay to others.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List the headcount, roles, pay and the month's attendance and leave data.
2. Calculate gross pay, statutory deductions (PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS) and net pay.
3. Calculate the employer side: employer PF, ESI, bonus, gratuity and other benefits.
4. Reconcile the payroll total with the bank payment file and the books.
5. Check due dates for PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax deposits and returns.
6. Plan the cost of new hires, raises and bonuses against the budget.
7. Flag errors: duplicate names, missing bank details, pay above or below the usual range.
8. Report the total cost of the workforce as a share of revenue.
9. Draft the payroll summary and notify the owner for approval.
