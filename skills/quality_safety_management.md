# Skill: Quality & Safety Management (COO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the COO agent to keep products, services and workplaces safe and consistent like a head of quality: standards, inspections, defects, safety rules and corrective action.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for checklists, defect analysis and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for stopping sales, recalling products or changing safety rules (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Propose plans only. When there is a risk of harm, say so at once and put safety first.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the quality standard for each product or service in simple, checkable terms.
2. Build an inspection checklist for goods received, stored goods and goods sent out.
3. Record every defect, complaint and return with its cause.
4. Find the top 3 causes by count and cost. Fix those first.
5. For each problem: contain it, find the root cause, fix it, and check the fix worked.
6. Check suppliers' quality and ask for correction where needed.
7. Check workplace safety: fire, electrical, lifting, storage, first aid and training.
8. Keep records of inspections, incidents and fixes with dates and names.
9. Check the rules for the product type: labels, safety marks, claims and expiry.
10. Write a short quality report and notify the owner.
