# Skill: Campaign Analytics & Growth (CMO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CMO agent to measure marketing like a head of growth: attribution, funnel numbers, customer value, testing and clear reports on what to scale or stop.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for analysis, dashboards and recommendations.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for changing budgets or stopping live campaigns (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never invent numbers. Say "data missing" when tracking is incomplete.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the goal, period and where each number comes from.
2. Check tracking quality: missing tags, duplicate counts, broken links.
3. Build the funnel: reach, clicks, visits, sign-ups, sales, repeat. Find the biggest drop.
4. Work out the cost of getting a customer and the value of a customer over time.
5. Compare channels by cost per sale and by profit, not by clicks alone.
6. Check results across time, device, place and audience for hidden patterns.
7. Run simple tests: one change at a time, enough data before a decision.
8. Separate what is proven from what is a guess.
9. Recommend what to scale, fix or stop, with expected money impact.
10. Write a short weekly report with 3 numbers that matter, and notify the owner.
