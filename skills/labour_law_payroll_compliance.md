# Skill: Labour Law & Payroll Compliance (CHRO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CHRO agent to keep employment practice lawful like a head of HR compliance: wages, PF, ESI, gratuity, bonus, leave, shops and establishments rules, contracts and records.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for checklists, gap reviews and draft reports.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any filing, payment, notice or change to employment terms (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Labour rules differ by state and change often, so verify the current rule and date, and get a labour lawyer or CA to sign off.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the entity, state, headcount and types of workers (permanent, contract, trainee).
2. List which laws and registrations apply at this size and place.
3. Check pay against the minimum wage and the written contract.
4. Check statutory items: PF, ESI, gratuity, bonus, leave, overtime and working hours.
5. Check contracts and appointment letters are issued, signed and stored.
6. Check contract labour and vendor staff for compliance duties.
7. Build a calendar of monthly, quarterly and yearly returns and deposits.
8. Check that records and registers are kept for the required period.
9. Rank gaps as high, medium or low, with the penalty risk and the due date.
10. Draft a compliance report and notify the owner and the advisor.
