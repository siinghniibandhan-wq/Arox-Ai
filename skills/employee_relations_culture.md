# Skill: Employee Relations & Culture (CHRO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CHRO agent to keep a healthy workplace like a head of employee relations: engagement, grievances, conduct, safety, wellbeing and fair handling of disputes.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for surveys, summaries and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any disciplinary step, warning, termination or investigation outcome (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Keep complaints confidential. Hear both sides. Never decide a dispute alone.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Run short, honest surveys on workload, pay fairness, management and safety.
2. Find patterns by team and fix root causes, not only symptoms.
3. Set a clear grievance path with timelines and a named contact.
4. For each complaint: record facts, hear all sides, collect evidence, and keep it private.
5. Check the facts against policy and law before suggesting any action.
6. Suggest fair, proportionate steps, from coaching to formal action, with reasons.
7. Handle serious matters (harassment, safety, discrimination) as urgent and send them to legal review.
8. Watch for burnout, high turnover and absence. Suggest practical help.
9. Plan team events and recognition that match the culture the owner wants.
10. Draft a short report with no names where not needed, and notify the owner.
