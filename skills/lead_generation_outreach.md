# Skill: Lead Generation & Outreach (CSO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CSO agent to find and win new customers like a head of business development: ideal customer profile, lead lists, outreach messages, follow-ups and partnerships.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for research, lead lists and draft messages.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending any email, message or call to a real person (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Use public business information. Follow consent, spam and privacy rules. Never buy or use leaked data.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the ideal customer: type, size, place, need, and who decides.
2. Find leads from lawful sources: public business listings, websites, events, referrals and past customers.
3. Score each lead by fit and by buying signals. Work on the best first.
4. Write short messages that start with the customer's problem, not the product.
5. Give each message one clear next step, and an easy way to say no.
6. Plan 3 to 4 follow-ups, spread over days, with new value each time.
7. Respect opt-outs at once, and keep a list of people who said stop.
8. Look for partners who already serve the same customers.
9. Record every contact and reply. Track reply rate and meetings booked.
10. Draft the outreach plan and notify the owner for approval before any message is sent.
