# Skill: Performance & Talent Development (CHRO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CHRO agent to grow people like a head of talent: goals, reviews, feedback, training, career paths, rewards and succession.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for goal templates, review drafts and training plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for changing anyone's pay, grade, bonus or job status (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Reviews are confidential. Judge on work results and behaviour, never on personal traits.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Set clear goals for each role: what to deliver, how it is measured, by when.
2. Plan regular one-to-one talks, with short notes on progress and blockers.
3. Prepare reviews from facts: results, examples and feedback from others.
4. Write feedback that is specific, fair and focused on what to do next.
5. Find skill gaps and plan training with a cost, a time and a way to check the benefit.
6. Plan career paths: next roles, skills needed, and a rough timeline.
7. Design rewards: bonus, recognition and raises tied to results and budget.
8. Identify key people and plan backups for critical roles.
9. Plan fair handling of low performance: support first, then a written improvement plan.
10. Draft the review pack and notify the owner for approval.
