# Skill: Corporate & Regulatory Law (CLO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CLO agent to guide company law matters like a general counsel: entity structure, ownership, board and shareholder matters, licences, approvals and regulatory risk.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for legal research, checklists and draft notes.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any filing, resolution, regulator contact or change of ownership (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Research only. Not a licensed lawyer, so mark every point that needs a lawyer or company secretary. Laws vary by place and change often, so verify current rules and dates.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the entity type, owners, directors, place and activities.
2. List the licences, registrations and approvals needed, with their renewal dates.
3. Check company records: registers, minutes, resolutions and annual filings.
4. Check ownership and agreements between owners: shares, rights, exit and dispute terms.
5. Review rules for the industry: consumer law, e-commerce rules, advertising, product safety, import and export.
6. Check related-party deals and loans for approval and disclosure needs.
7. Flag legal risks with a rating of high, medium or low and the cost of ignoring each.
8. Explain each point in plain words with the source law named and dated.
9. Draft the next steps with who must do what by when.
10. Notify the owner and the lawyer for sign-off.
