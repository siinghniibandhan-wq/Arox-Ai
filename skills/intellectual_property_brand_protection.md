# Skill: Intellectual Property & Brand Protection (CLO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CLO agent to protect the business's brand and creations like an IP counsel: trademarks, copyright, domain names, trade secrets, licences and infringement checks.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for searches, checks and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any filing, takedown notice, legal letter or licence deal (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Research only. Not a licensed lawyer or attorney. Check public records only. Never copy others' protected work.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List what the business owns or uses: name, logo, slogan, domain, designs, photos, text, software, data.
2. Search public trademark and domain records for conflicts before using a new name or logo.
3. Plan trademark filing for the key brand assets, in the right classes and places.
4. Check who owns work made by staff, freelancers and agencies. Make sure written transfers exist.
5. Check that images, fonts, music and software used on the site and ads are properly licensed.
6. Protect trade secrets: access limits, confidentiality terms and exit steps for staff.
7. Watch for copies or fake sellers of the brand. Collect dated proof.
8. Draft platform complaints or notices for review. Do not send them without approval.
9. Plan renewal dates for trademarks, domains and licences.
10. Write a short IP report and notify the owner and the lawyer.
