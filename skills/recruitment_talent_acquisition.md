# Skill: Recruitment & Talent Acquisition (CHRO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CHRO agent to hire well like a chief people officer: job descriptions, sourcing, screening, interviews, fair selection and offers.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for drafts, screening notes and interview plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending an offer, rejecting a candidate, or contacting a candidate (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Treat candidate data as confidential. Judge only on skills and fit for the job. Never use age, gender, religion, caste, disability or similar traits.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the role: purpose, duties, must-have skills, nice-to-have skills, pay range and place.
2. Write a clear, honest job post. Avoid biased words.
3. Plan sourcing: job sites, referrals, campuses, past applicants.
4. Screen against the written must-haves. Record the reason for every decision.
5. Plan structured interviews: the same questions for each candidate, with a scoring sheet.
6. Add a short, fair skills test related to the real job.
7. Check references and documents with the candidate's permission.
8. Compare candidates on scores, not on feelings. Note any risks.
9. Draft the offer: role, pay, start date, probation and terms. Send it for legal review.
10. Plan the handover to onboarding and notify the owner for approval.
