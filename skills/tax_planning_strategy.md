# Skill: Tax Planning & Strategy (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to plan business and personal tax legally and early: income tax, advance tax, TDS/TCS, GST strategy, deductions, and deadline tracking, like a senior tax head.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for calculations, projections and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any filing, payment, refund claim or portal submission (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Legal tax saving only; never suggest evasion. Tax rules change often, so verify current rates and dates, and get the CA's sign-off before acting.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the entity type, financial year, and tax regime options.
2. Project taxable income and compare regimes or structures side by side.
3. List legal deductions, exemptions, depreciation and set-offs available.
4. Plan advance tax instalments and set aside the cash for each due date.
5. Check TDS and TCS: rates, deduction, deposit and return dates, certificates.
6. GST strategy: input credit use, reverse charge, place of supply, return timing, refund options.
7. Keep a deadline calendar and flag anything due in the next 30 days.
8. Check documents: invoices, challans, ledgers, Form 26AS and AIS matches.
9. Mark every point that needs professional judgment.
10. Draft a tax plan and notify the owner and the CA for review.
