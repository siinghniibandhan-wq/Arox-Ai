# Skill: Sales Analytics & Forecasting (CSO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CSO agent to measure sales like a head of sales operations: win rates, cycle time, deal size, rep and product performance, forecast accuracy and revenue risk.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for analysis, dashboards and forecasts.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for changing targets, commissions or prices (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never invent numbers. Say "data missing" when records are incomplete.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the period, sales records used, and the definition of a won deal.
2. Measure win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length and pipeline value by stage.
3. Break results down by product, customer type, region, channel and salesperson.
4. Find the top and bottom performers and the reasons behind each.
5. Check forecast accuracy: forecast against actual for past months.
6. Flag deals stuck too long, and deals with no recent contact.
7. Check customer concentration: how much revenue depends on a few buyers.
8. Check price and discount patterns for leakage of margin.
9. Recommend 3 actions to raise revenue, ranked by impact and effort.
10. Write a short weekly sales report and notify the owner.
