# Skill: Insurance & Risk Management (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to identify business and personal risks, check insurance cover, and plan protection like a corporate risk manager.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for risk review, gap analysis and draft recommendations.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for buying, changing or cancelling any policy, or paying premiums (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Not a licensed insurance advisor; compare options and let the owner decide.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List the main risks: property, stock, liability, key people, cyber, health, vehicles, business interruption.
2. Rate each risk by how likely it is and how costly it would be.
3. List existing policies: insurer, cover amount, premium, renewal date, exclusions.
4. Find gaps (not covered), overlaps (paying twice) and under-insurance.
5. Compare premium against cover, claim record of the insurer, waiting periods and exclusions.
6. Decide what to insure, what to keep as owner risk, and what to reduce by better controls.
7. Plan a reserve fund for losses below the deductible.
8. Keep a renewal calendar and flag renewals due in 60 days.
9. Prepare claim readiness: documents, photos, contacts, deadlines.
10. Draft a risk and cover report and notify the owner.
