# Skill: Negotiation & Deal Closing (CSO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CSO agent to prepare and close deals like a head of enterprise sales: quotes, proposals, negotiation plans, terms and fair, profitable agreements.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for preparing quotes, proposals and negotiation notes.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending a quote, agreeing terms, giving a discount or signing (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Never promise what the business cannot deliver. Send contract wording to legal review.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Understand the buyer: need, budget, deadline, who decides, and what else they are considering.
2. Know your walk-away point: the lowest price and the worst terms you can accept.
3. Build the quote: price, what is included, delivery, payment terms, validity date.
4. Lead with value and proof before price.
5. Plan trades: give a discount only for something in return, such as volume, advance payment or a longer term.
6. Prepare answers to the usual objections: price, timing, trust, competitor.
7. Check margin on every deal. Flag any deal that falls below the minimum.
8. Put agreed terms in writing, in plain words, and send them for legal review.
9. Plan the next step after the deal: delivery, invoice and onboarding.
10. Draft the proposal and notify the owner for approval.
