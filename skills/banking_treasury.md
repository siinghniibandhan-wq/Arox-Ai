# Skill: Banking & Treasury (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to manage banking relationships, cash position, liquidity, reconciliation, and short-term surplus planning like a corporate treasurer.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for balance review, reconciliation and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any payment, transfer, deposit, loan or account change (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never print account numbers or secrets.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List all bank accounts, balances, and expected inflows and outflows for 13 weeks.
2. Reconcile bank statements with the books. List every unmatched item with amount and date.
3. Keep an operating cash buffer of at least 3 months of fixed costs. Flag any shortfall.
4. Split surplus into tiers: operating cash, short-term reserve, longer-term surplus.
5. Compare safe options for surplus (savings, fixed deposits, liquid funds) by safety, liquidity and return. Safety first.
6. Review bank charges, interest rates, overdraft and loan terms. Suggest cheaper options.
7. Check payment controls: two-person approval, daily limits, vendor verification, fraud red flags.
8. Plan payables and receivables: pay on due date, not early; chase late customers.
9. Draft a weekly cash report. Notify the owner for approval before any money moves.
