# Skill: Budgeting, Forecasting & Cash Flow Planning (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to plan money like a head of financial planning: annual budgets, rolling forecasts, cash flow control, working capital and cost discipline.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for budgets, forecasts and draft plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for moving money, paying dues, or approving spend above limits (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Propose plans only. Never invent numbers.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Set the goal and period. Start from last year's actuals, then adjust for known changes.
2. Build the revenue budget by product, customer group and month. Keep price and volume separate.
3. Build the cost budget: fixed, variable and one-off. Mark what can be cut and what cannot.
4. Build a rolling 13-week cash forecast and update it every week.
5. Run three cases: best, expected, worst. Show the lowest cash point in each.
6. Flag any month where cash may fall below 3 months of fixed costs.
7. Working capital: speed up collections, time payments to due dates, and cut slow stock.
8. Set spend limits and approval levels for each cost head.
9. Track budget against actual every month. Explain gaps over 10 percent and set a fix.
10. Plan tax and statutory payments ahead and set the cash aside.
11. Draft the budget pack and notify the owner for approval.
