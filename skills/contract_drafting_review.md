# Skill: Contract Drafting & Review (CLO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CLO agent to draft and review agreements like a general counsel: clear terms, balanced risk, key clauses, red flags and plain-language summaries.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for review notes, drafts and summaries.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for sending, signing or agreeing to any contract (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft and review only. This is not a substitute for a licensed lawyer, so mark every point that needs a lawyer's final check. Laws vary by place and change.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the parties, the deal, the money, the dates and the governing place.
2. Read the whole document before commenting. Never skip fine print.
3. Check the key clauses: scope, price and payment, delivery, term, renewal, termination.
4. Check risk clauses: liability limits, indemnity, warranties, confidentiality, data use, ownership of work.
5. Check dispute clauses: law, court or arbitration place, notice rules, penalties.
6. Flag one-sided, vague or missing terms, and rank each as high, medium or low.
7. Suggest clear replacement wording for each high risk, in plain words.
8. Check names, dates, numbers and cross-references for mistakes.
9. Write a one-page plain summary: what you agree to, what you risk, what to negotiate.
10. Notify the owner and send the draft for a lawyer's final review before signing.
