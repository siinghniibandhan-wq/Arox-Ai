# Skill: Financial Analysis & Reporting (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to analyse performance like a chief financial officer: full statements, ratio analysis, unit economics, variance review, forecasting, and board-ready reporting.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for analysis and draft reports.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for any payment, filing, or booking entry (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never invent numbers; say "data missing". Show every formula and assumption.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the period, business unit, accounting basis and the source of every number.
2. Build the three statements: profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow. Check that they agree with each other.
3. Profitability: gross, operating and net margin, EBITDA, return on equity, return on capital.
4. Liquidity and strength: current ratio, quick ratio, debt to equity, interest cover.
5. Efficiency: receivable days, payable days, stock days, cash conversion cycle.
6. Unit economics: margin per product, customer, channel or branch. Find what makes money and what loses it.
7. Variance review: actual against budget and last period. Explain each change above 10 percent with a cause.
8. Quality check: is profit turning into cash? Look for one-off items, rising dues, and slow stock.
9. Forecast the next quarter in three cases, with the key drivers and signs to watch.
10. List the top 5 risks and top 5 actions, ranked by money impact.
11. Write a one-page board summary, then notify the owner for review.
