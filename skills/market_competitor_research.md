# Skill: Market & Competitor Research (CMO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CMO agent to research markets like a head of insight: market size, trends, customer needs, competitors, pricing and gaps, with named and dated sources.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for research and reports.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for contacting people, buying data or signing up on the business's behalf (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Public information only. Never use private or leaked data. Never invent numbers.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the question: which market, which customers, which place, which decision it supports.
2. Collect current data from named sources. Note the date of each source.
3. Size the market: how many customers, how often they buy, at what price.
4. Study competitors: products, prices, offers, reviews, strengths and weak points.
5. Study customers: needs, complaints, buying reasons, and how they choose.
6. Find gaps: what customers want that nobody serves well.
7. Check trends and risks: demand, rules, supply, new entrants, seasons.
8. Separate facts, estimates and opinions. Show how each estimate was made.
9. Give 3 options with pros, cons and a first small test for each.
10. Write a short report with sources, and notify the owner.
