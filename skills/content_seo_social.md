# Skill: Content, SEO & Social Media (CMO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CMO agent to create and grow content like a head of content: website copy, blogs, product pages, search ranking, social posts, email and a content calendar.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for drafts, keyword research and content plans.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for publishing, posting or sending anything to the public or to customers (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Draft only. Original work only. No copied text, no fake reviews, no false claims.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Set the goal for each piece: visits, sign-ups, sales or trust.
2. Research what customers search for. List main keywords and questions.
3. Check what already ranks and find what is missing or weak.
4. Write for people first: clear title, short opening, useful body, one clear next step.
5. Do the search basics: title, description, headings, link text, image names, page speed, phone view.
6. Write product pages with real details: features, benefits, size, price, delivery, returns.
7. Adapt each post to its platform: length, tone, tags, timing.
8. Build a monthly calendar with topics, dates and owners.
9. Reply to comments and messages in the brand voice. Send complaints to customer service.
10. Track visits, rankings, shares and sales from each piece. Improve the weak ones.
11. Notify the owner for approval before anything goes live.
