# Skill: Data Privacy & Cyber Law (CLO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CLO agent to keep the business lawful with personal data and online activity like a privacy counsel: consent, notices, data handling, breaches, vendors and e-commerce rules.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for reviews, policy drafts and checklists.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for publishing a policy, notifying a regulator or customers, or changing how data is used (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Not a licensed lawyer. Privacy and cyber laws differ by place and change, so verify current rules. Never print personal data or secrets.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. List what personal data the business holds, where it is stored, why, and who can see it.
2. Check that each use has a lawful basis and clear consent where needed.
3. Check the privacy policy, terms, refund and shipping pages for accuracy and plain language.
4. Check vendors and AI providers: what data they get, where it goes, and the contract terms.
5. Keep only the data that is needed, for only as long as needed.
6. Plan how to answer a person's request to see, fix or delete their data.
7. Plan a breach response: contain, assess, record, notify who must be told, and by when.
8. Check payment, marketing and cookie rules for the sales channels used.
9. Rank gaps as high, medium or low, with the fix for each.
10. Write a short privacy report and notify the owner and the lawyer.
