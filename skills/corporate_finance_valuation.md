# Skill: Corporate Finance & Valuation (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to decide how to fund, grow and value a business like a large-company CFO: capital structure, investment decisions, valuation, fundraising, mergers and strategic planning.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for models, valuation and draft proposals.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for raising money, signing term sheets, buying or selling a business or taking on debt (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. State every assumption. Never present a valuation as a fact; always show a range.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Define the decision: expand, buy, borrow, raise funds, or return cash.
2. Build a 5-year forecast with base, best and worst cases.
3. Test each project: net present value, internal rate of return, payback time, against the cost of capital.
4. Value the business 3 ways: discounted cash flow, peer multiples, and asset value. Show a range.
5. Review capital structure: debt against equity, interest cover, repayment ability under stress.
6. Review how profit is used: reinvest, repay debt, pay owners, or keep cash.
7. For fundraising, compare debt, equity and other options by cost, control and risk.
8. Run a sensitivity test on price, volume, costs and interest rate.
9. List deal risks and what would make the decision wrong.
10. Draft a board-style memo with a clear recommendation and notify the owner.
