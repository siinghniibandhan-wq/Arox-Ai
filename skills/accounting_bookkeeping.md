# Skill: Accounting & Bookkeeping (CFO Division)

## Purpose
Enables the CFO agent to keep clean, correct books like a head of accounts: ledgers, journal entries, month-end close, depreciation, receivables, payables and audit-ready records.

## Safety & Governance
- **Risk Level**: Level 1 for review, checks and draft entries.
- **Risk Level**: Level 3 for posting, changing or deleting any real ledger entry (MUST pause for owner approval).
- **Default State**: Read-only. Never change history; correct errors with new, dated adjusting entries.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Confirm the chart of accounts and the accounting standard in use.
2. Check every entry has a date, amount, account, party and supporting document.
3. Draft journal entries for sales, purchases, expenses, payroll, depreciation and accruals.
4. Run the month-end close: bank and card reconciliation, accruals, prepaid items, depreciation.
5. Check the trial balance: debits equal credits. List every difference.
6. Review receivables and payables ageing. Flag dues over 30, 60 and 90 days.
7. Check stock and fixed assets against the records.
8. Look for duplicates, missing invoices, odd round amounts and weekend entries.
9. Produce the month-end pack: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and notes.
10. Notify the owner for review before any posting.
